Rakshak Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Rakshak is a team safety and coordination app. A commander sees the status, position and messages of the field units in their team, and field units can send an SOS. This page explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your role (commander or field unit) and your team. A commander creates your account.
  • Location (field units only): your precise location, collected while you are signed in, including in the background when the app is closed or the screen is off. It is shown to your commander on the team map. You are asked for permission before collection starts, and a notification is visible while it is on.
  • Battery level (field units only): shown to your commander so they know when a device may go offline.
  • Messages, alerts and orders: the chat messages, SOS alerts, pings and redeploy orders you send or receive in the app.
  • Push token: an identifier for your phone so the app can send you notifications.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: crash reports with device model, Android version and an anonymous account id, to fix bugs.

How it is used

Only to run the app: show your team its members, deliver alerts and messages, and keep the app stable. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or share it with advertisers.

Who processes it

Data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging and Crashlytics) on our behalf. Your data is visible to the commanders and field units in your own team through the app.

How long we keep it

  • Alerts and chat messages are deleted automatically after 7 days. Ping and order records are deleted after 1 day.
  • Your latest position and battery level are overwritten with every update; they are not kept as a history.
  • Account details are kept until your account is deleted.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app go to Settings, then "Delete my account". This deletes your login and profile. You can also email us at the address below and we will delete it for you.
  • Stop location sharing: sign out, or turn off location for Rakshak in Android Settings.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app (Settings) or in Android Settings.

Children

Rakshak is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and access rules allow each person to see and change only what their role permits. No system is perfectly secure.
